Principle

How It Works

01

Light Source

A high-brightness lamp generates a powerful, focused beam of white light inside the enclosure.

02

LCD Panel

Light passes through an LCD screen which acts as the "slide" — displaying the image frame by frame.

03

Projection Lens

A convex lens magnifies the image from the LCD and projects it onto a wall or screen at distance.

04

Thermal Control

Filtered intake fans and an exhaust system keep the unit cool, preventing heat damage to components.

Process

Build Process

01

Planning

Designed the internal layout and marked slates for precise cutting.

02

Enclosure

Cut and assembled the custom housing box from marked slates.

03

Lens Fitting

Mounted and positioned the projection lens in the front of the enclosure.

04

Foundation

Built the internal structural base to hold all components securely.

05

Electricals & Exhaust

Installed wiring, power routing, and the ventilation exhaust outlet.

06

Light Source Wiring

Wired up the high-brightness lamp with proper connections and safety.

07

Optics Alignment

Fine-tuned the lamp and lens positions for optimal image clarity and brightness.

08

LCD Screen

Fitted the display panel into the enclosure in the optical path.

09

Air Intake Filter

Added a filtered intake to keep dust out while allowing airflow.

10

Temperature Control

Implemented a thermal management system to monitor and manage heat.
